# 04: Showcase layout and card content

**Spec:** [011-projects-showcase](../spec.md)

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** ready-for-human

## What to build

The static showcase: the featured card, the two smaller cards, and the new card content. No tilt and no magnetic pull yet (ticket 05); this ticket has to look finished on its own, because that is what touch devices and reduced-motion visitors will see. Files: `apps/web/components/projects/project-grid.tsx`, `projects.tsx`, `apps/web/lib/projects.ts`.

- **Layout.** The grid stays data-driven from `getProjects()`. The first Project by `order` is the featured card and spans the full row from `md` up; the rest sit in two columns below it. Nothing refers to a slug or a name. On mobile everything is one column in the same order. The featured card may be laid out wider than a normal one (cover and text side by side, or a taller cover); this is a proposal from the spec, so look at it at 1440, 1024 and 768px, compare it against an even three-column grid, and keep whichever looks better. Write the decision and the reason under Comments.
- **HUD index.** `[01]`, `[02]`, `[03]` from `order`. One format everywhere: `formatProjectNumber` in `lib/projects.ts` now returns `[01]` (it used to return `_01.`), and the card and the Case study Open Graph watermark both use it. No second formatter.
- **Commercial / Personal marker.** `ProjectCard` gets the `type` field and the card shows a clear marker from it, using the existing `projectTypeLabel`. Commercial gets the Ember accent treatment, Personal a neutral one; both text colours pass AA on the card surface.
- **Meta line.** `formatProjectMeta` starts with the type ("Commercial · Anthill · 2023 — Present"), so the marker and the meta would say "Commercial" twice. On the card, the meta line shows the remainder (Employer and year) and the marker carries the type. `formatProjectMeta` itself is left as it is for the Case study and the OG image.
- **Rest of the card.** Name, summary, up to four stack tags with the `+N` overflow as today, and an "Open Case study" affordance at the bottom of the card. The affordance is a non-interactive element inside the existing single `Link` (no nested link or button); ticket 05 wraps it in `MagneticButton`.
- **Cover.** The covers from spec 008 stay. The frame gets one consistent aspect ratio and crop for every card, and on hover and keyboard focus an Ember glow on the border (a border colour plus a soft box-shadow in the accent). `next/image` stays; `sizes` is set per card to the width it really renders at (the featured card is the full content column, the others half of it); only the first card has `priority`. The existing hover zoom on the image stays and is still off under reduced motion.
- **Entrance.** The stagger and `fadeUp` on each `li` stay as they are.
- **Heading.** The Section keeps `SectionHeading` with the label set in ticket 01.

## Comments

- Done: `project-grid.tsx`, `projects.tsx`, `lib/projects.ts`. `ProjectCard` now carries `type` and `typeLabel` (from `projectTypeLabel`, resolved in the server component), and `meta` is the new `formatProjectCardMeta` (Employer and year, e.g. "Anthill · 2023 — Present"; just "2026" for a Personal project). `formatProjectMeta` is untouched, so the Case study page and the OG image still use it. `formatProjectNumber` already returned `[01]` from an earlier commit and is the only formatter; the card and the OG watermark both use it.
- Layout decision: keep the featured card. The first Project by `order` (`index === 0`, no slug or name anywhere) spans both columns from `md` and lays out cover and text side by side from `lg` (cover `lg:w-[45%]`, text takes the rest) and stacks cover over text between `md` and `lg`; every other card is a half-width column below it, and everything is one column under `md`. I compared it with an even three-column grid at 1024px (the grid was forced in the DOM, not committed): the cards drop to 304px wide, the covers become unreadable thumbnails, the description and tags wrap into tall narrow columns, and nothing says that one Project is the commercial one. The featured card has a clear first place, and at 1024 and 1440 it is a short wide band, and stacks cleanly at 375 and 320. A fourth Project would simply be a third card in the second row.
- Card content: HUD index `[01]` and the type marker share the top row; the marker is a pill, Commercial in Ember (`border-accent/40 bg-accent/10 text-accent`), Personal neutral (`border-border bg-foreground/5 text-muted`). Both text colours are around 6:1 on the card surface by estimate (not run through a checker). Under the marker row: name (larger on the featured card), the Employer and year line, summary, up to four tags with `+N`, and "View project →" as a plain `span` at the bottom of the card, inside the existing single `Link`. The arrow nudges right on hover, and not under reduced motion. Ticket 05 wraps this span in `MagneticButton`.
- Cover and glow: the regular cards keep `aspect-video` with `object-cover object-top-left` (all covers are 16:9, so nothing is cropped). The featured cover first kept `aspect-video` too and stopped short of the card bottom, leaving an empty strip under it, which looked like a cropped, floating image; it now drops the ratio from `lg` and fills the card height, anchored top-left (the sidebar side). First tried 62% of the card, which read as more than half the card; 45% lets the text column widen and shorten, so the card is 314px tall at 1440 and the cover (438x312) still shows about 79% of the 16:9 image. The same 45% at 768 cropped to 48% of the image, so between `md` and `lg` the featured card stacks (cover full width, uncropped) instead. This is the one cover that deviates from the shared aspect ratio. The Ember glow is on the card's own border, not the cover frame: border colour goes to accent and a `0 0 40px -12px var(--accent)` shadow fades in on hover and on `focus-visible`, next to the existing 2px accent outline. Checked: hover read `rgb(255, 107, 61)` border and the accent shadow; a real Tab gave `:focus-visible` true, the solid 2px accent outline, and the same border and shadow after the 300ms transition. The image zoom is unchanged and still off under reduced motion.
- Images: `priority` only on the featured card (the others are `loading="lazy"`). `sizes` now matches the rendered widths: featured is 440px at 1024+, the full column below; the others are 476px at 1024+, half the column from 768, the full column below (the old `100vw` fallback was wider than the real box). Served widths against rendered: 640 for 564 at 1440 (featured), 640 for 474 (others), 640 for 407 at 768, 750 for 325 on the 375 viewport (that viewport reports a density above 1). That is the nearest size Next generates, not far above.
- Checked in the browser at 1440, 1024, 768, 375 and 320: no horizontal scroll at any of them (and no descendant wider than the viewport at 320); the list holds three links, one tab stop each, and no `a a` or `a button` nesting. The first card spans 976px at 1440 with the other two at 476px side by side.
- Copy: the card affordance first read "Open Case study", which a visitor can take for a learning exercise, odd on a commercial product. It now reads "View project →". "Case study" stays the domain term for the page in `CONTEXT.md`; nothing else in the UI uses the words. One line in `project-grid.tsx` to change back.
- Header (found while reviewing, not part of this ticket): the pill had `pr-3` on the right, sized for the mobile menu button, so on `md` and up the links sat 12px from the edge against 24px on the left. Added `md:pr-6` in `header.tsx`; at 1024 the name is 25px from the left edge and Contact 25px from the right.
- Not checked: layout shift while images load. The cover is a `fill` image inside an `aspect-video` frame, so the box is reserved from the first paint, but I did not measure CLS (Lighthouse is in ticket 06). The OG watermark was checked by code, not by rendering the image: it uses the same `formatProjectNumber`, and the Case study page is untouched.
